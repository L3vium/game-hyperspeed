# Environment assets

All rocks are Poly Haven scans (CC0, no attribution required, credited anyway), downloaded through the
Blender MCP addon, decimated in Blender (boulders ~2400 tris + `_lo` ~420 tris, cliffs ~5000 tris) and exported
with their 1k albedo. In game they are merged per chunk by `RockKit` and drawn with the Hyperspeed toon shader.
